#### 2.13
bis(x, y)  
bis(x, y) - bic(0xFFFFFFFF, bis(~x, ~y))  

解法一：  
x^y = (x|y)-(x&y)  
= (x|y)-(~(~x|~y))

1000^1001 = 0001  
= (1000|1001)-(~(0111|0110))  
= 1001-(~0111)  
= 1001-1000
= 0001

x|y = bis(x, y)  
x&y = ~(~x|~y)  
= bic(0xFFFFFFFF, bis(~x, ~y))  

解法二：  
x^y = x&~y | y&~x

1000^1001 = 0001  
= 1000&0110 | 1001&0111
= 0000 | 0001
= 0001

bic(x, y) = x&~y   
= bis(bic(x,y), bic(y,x))
